# HAL market photography shot list

Purpose: replace the temporary editorial illustrations on [[Hoi An Market Guide]] with HAL-owned photography. Recognizable faces are not required. Ask permission when a person, their hands or their stall becomes the clear subject rather than incidental background.

## Priority replacements

- [ ] Wide morning establishing shot: clear market aisle, produce and working context; avoid blocking traffic.
- [ ] Vegetable and herb stall: several distinguishable herb bundles, natural working arrangement.
- [ ] Vietnamese herbs close-up: record the names only when confirmed by the vendor or a knowledgeable guide.
- [ ] Green papaya: whole and, if naturally available, prepared or shredded context.
- [ ] Banana blossom: whole flower and prepared slices if naturally available.
- [ ] Fresh turmeric: only when available; do not stage or mislabel another rhizome.
- [ ] Noodle stall: visually distinguish cao lau, Mi Quang or other noodles only after confirming their names.
- [ ] Fish and seafood area: respectful wide or hands-only frame; avoid graphic close-ups.
- [ ] Fish sauce and condiment context: capture unlabeled containers only with permission; record product names separately.
- [ ] Vendor hands weighing produce: ask permission because the hands are the primary subject.
- [ ] Customer/vendor interaction: ask permission from both parties if either is identifiable.
- [ ] Working market aisle: show movement and scale without obstructing it.
- [ ] Ingredient selection: weighing, trimming or comparing produce; ask before photographing closely.
- [ ] Morning atmosphere: exterior or transitional scene that honestly reflects the time photographed.

## Capture standard

- Shoot horizontal 3:2 and leave crop room around hands, baskets and ingredients.
- Prefer natural light and truthful color; avoid heavy HDR or artificial lantern effects.
- Record date, approximate time, permission status and confirmed ingredient names.
- Avoid readable personal information, unrelated branding and identifiable children.
- Do not describe any photo as “early morning” unless its capture time supports that claim.

## Cao lau guide replacements

Purpose: replace the four temporary illustrations on [[Cao Lau in Hoi An]] with HAL-owned, accurately captioned photography.

- [ ] Finished bowl, horizontal 3:2: noodles, pork, herbs, sprouts and crisp elements all visible; record restaurant and permission.
- [ ] Complete bowl overhead: all principal components visible without styling it into an inaccurate recipe.
- [ ] Complete bowl at 45 degrees: leave crop room for 3:2 editorial use.
- [ ] Noodle close-up: show thickness and surface texture without claiming a preparation method the photograph cannot prove.
- [ ] Pork detail: record the vendor and confirm the serving is cao lau.
- [ ] Herb detail: photograph only ingredients that are present naturally and record confirmed names.
- [ ] Crispy topping detail: capture before it softens; do not assume one universal topping.
- [ ] Sauce visible at the bottom: enough to explain the low-liquid structure without restyling the bowl.
- [ ] Bowl before mixing: capture promptly after service.
- [ ] Mixed bowl: sauce coating the noodles with little free liquid; capture before the crisp elements become soft.
- [ ] Complete bite or lifted noodles: keep hands anonymous unless permission is recorded.
- [ ] Separate ingredient context: noodles, herbs, sprouts, pork and crisp rice elements only when their identity is confirmed.
- [ ] Preparation detail: steaming, cutting or assembly only with kitchen permission and an accurate process note.
- [ ] Cao lau noodles before serving: confirm the noodle with the vendor or cook.
- [ ] Ba Le well exterior or water collection only after location verification and permission; do not imply the photographed water supplied a specific bowl.

Capture date, approximate time, restaurant or kitchen, permission status and whether each ingredient was confirmed. Avoid readable third-party branding, staged folklore claims and identifiable children.

## Banh xeo guide replacements

Purpose: replace the four temporary illustrations on [[Banh Xeo in Hoi An]] with HAL-owned photography while preserving an accurate eating sequence.

- [ ] Pancake immediately after cooking: photograph before steam softens the shell.
- [ ] Pan/cooking action: show batter setting and edges crisping; obtain kitchen permission.
- [ ] Full serving setup: pancake, herbs, vegetables, wrapper and sauce visible separately.
- [ ] Herbs: confirm names when possible; do not imply one universal mix.
- [ ] Rice paper or wrapper: photograph what the restaurant actually serves.
- [ ] Dipping sauce: record the vendor's ingredient description without claiming a universal recipe.
- [ ] Filling close-up: shrimp, pork, sprouts or other ingredients only when confirmed.
- [ ] Hands assembling a wrap: permission required when hands are the clear subject.
- [ ] Completed roll: show a manageable ratio of pancake, herbs and vegetables.
- [ ] Dipping action: capture a light dip without restaging the sauce.
- [ ] Bite showing crisp pancake and herbs: record whether it was photographed immediately after cooking.
- [ ] Wide local dining context: avoid identifiable faces unless permission is recorded.
- [ ] Cook preparing the pancake: obtain explicit permission and keep the pan workflow unobstructed.

Keep original high-resolution files. Record date, approximate time, location, permission status, confirmed ingredients and whether any food was restyled for the image.
