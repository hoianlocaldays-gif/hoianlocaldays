# HAL market photography shot list

Purpose: replace the temporary editorial illustrations on [[Hoi An Market Guide]] with HAL-owned photography. Recognizable faces are not required. Ask permission when a person, their hands or their stall becomes the clear subject rather than incidental background.

## Priority replacements

- [ ] Wide morning establishing shot: clear market aisle, produce and working context; avoid blocking traffic.
- [ ] Vegetable and herb stall: several distinguishable herb bundles, natural working arrangement.
- [ ] Vietnamese herbs close-up: record the names only when confirmed by the vendor or a knowledgeable guide.
- [ ] Green papaya: whole and, if naturally available, prepared or shredded context.
- [ ] Banana blossom: whole flower and prepared slices if naturally available.
- [ ] Fresh turmeric: only when available; do not stage or mislabel another rhizome.
- [ ] Noodle stall: visually distinguish cao lau, Mi Quang or other noodles only after confirming their names.
- [ ] Fish and seafood area: respectful wide or hands-only frame; avoid graphic close-ups.
- [ ] Fish sauce and condiment context: capture unlabeled containers only with permission; record product names separately.
- [ ] Vendor hands weighing produce: ask permission because the hands are the primary subject.
- [ ] Customer/vendor interaction: ask permission from both parties if either is identifiable.
- [ ] Working market aisle: show movement and scale without obstructing it.
- [ ] Ingredient selection: weighing, trimming or comparing produce; ask before photographing closely.
- [ ] Morning atmosphere: exterior or transitional scene that honestly reflects the time photographed.

## Capture standard

- Shoot horizontal 3:2 and leave crop room around hands, baskets and ingredients.
- Prefer natural light and truthful color; avoid heavy HDR or artificial lantern effects.
- Record date, approximate time, permission status and confirmed ingredient names.
- Avoid readable personal information, unrelated branding and identifiable children.
- Do not describe any photo as “early morning” unless its capture time supports that claim.

- [ ] Finished bowl, horizontal 3:2: noodles, pork, herbs, sprouts and crisp elements all visible; record restaurant and permission.

- [ ] Complete bite or lifted noodles: keep hands anonymous unless permission is recorded.
- [ ] Separate ingredient context: noodles, herbs, sprouts, pork and crisp rice elements only when their identity is confirmed.

Capture date, approximate time, restaurant or kitchen, permission status and whether each ingredient was confirmed. Avoid readable third-party branding, staged folklore claims and identifiable children.
